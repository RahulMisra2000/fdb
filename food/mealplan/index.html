Night before

What to soak each night. The list at the top follows the shared plan, so if anyone changes tomorrow's meals, it updates.

Rajma and chickpeas

about ⅓ cup dry per bowl3 times their volume of water

Soak overnight, 8-12 hours. In the morning, drain, rinse and pressure-cook in fresh water.

Discard the water Soaking and draining removes some of the phytates that block iron and zinc, and the sugars that cause gas.

Black-eyed peas and arhar dal

Black-eyed peas need 6-8 hours (overnight is fine). Arhar dal needs only 30-60 minutes before cooking, so it doesn't show up in tonight's list.

Whole moong for sprouts

about ⅓ cup (68 g) dry moong makes about 1½ cups of sprouts.

Soak overnight, drain in the morning, then keep it covered in a colander or damp cloth for about a day, rinsing morning and night. It's ready when the tails are just showing.

Chia

2 heaped tbsp chia seeds (about 25 g)water to cover well

Soak in a covered jar in the fridge, then stir it all into the yogurt drink with 2 tbsp ground flax.

Almonds and walnuts

21 almonds (26 g)7 walnut halves (14 g)water to cover

Soak overnight at room temperature.

Discard the water Rinse them. Peeling almonds is the Ayurvedic custom. Keeping the skin keeps its polyphenols.

Omelette days: for vitamin D, set fresh shiitake gill-side up in direct sun for 30-60 minutes before cooking, or use dried shiitake soaked in water. Only UV-exposed mushrooms make meaningful vitamin D, and the amount varies widely.

Smoothie

Why no banana or apple: both are high in polyphenol oxidase (PPO), the enzyme that browns cut fruit. Once blended, it destroys the flavanols in berries and grapes. Kale is low in PPO, and orange, mango and pineapple are the low-PPO fruits the researchers recommend pairing with berries.

If you make a full batch: refrigerate the rest in a full, sealed jar and drink it within 24 hours. Vitamin C fades with time and air, and Ayurveda advises against leftover food.

Food synergy

Why the vitamins and minerals in this plan are absorbed better than they would be from the same foods eaten in any order.

Vitamin C unlocks plant iron

lemonorangestrawberriespineapplekale with iron from greenspumpkin seedschiaoats

Plant iron is mostly ferric (Fe³⁺), which the gut absorbs poorly. Vitamin C converts it to ferrous iron (Fe²⁺) and keeps it soluble, which partly overcomes the phytates in nuts, seeds and oats.

Source: NIH ODS, Iron fact sheet

Fat carries fat-soluble vitamins

almondspeanutspumpkin seedsegg yolk with carotenoids and vitamins K and E in kalebok choy

These vitamins need fat to be absorbed. Monounsaturated fat, the main fat in almonds and peanuts, gave the same carotenoid absorption at 3 g as at 20 g. Whole eggs raised carotenoid absorption from raw vegetables 3- to 8-fold, but that was with 3 eggs, so 1 egg adds a smaller boost.

Source: Purdue, Goltz & Ferruzzi 2012; Kim et al. 2015, 2016

Low-enzyme smoothie protects flavanols

blueberriesgrapes with kaleorangepineapplemango, no banana or apple

The browning enzyme PPO destroys flavanols after blending. A banana smoothie cut flavanol absorption by 84% compared with a low-PPO berry smoothie.

Source: Ottaviani et al., Food & Function 2023. Small study, funded by Mars, Inc.

Calcium kept away from iron

Greek yogurtchia eaten 2 hours after the iron-rich smoothie

Calcium reduces iron absorption when the two are eaten in the same meal. Separating them protects the iron.

Source: NIH ODS, Iron fact sheet

Low-oxalate greens release their calcium

kalebok choy instead of spinach

Oxalate locks up calcium. Kale and bok choy are low in oxalate, so about 49–54% of their calcium is absorbed, compared with about 32% from milk and about 5% from spinach.

Source: Heaney & Weaver, Am J Clin Nutr 1990; Weaver et al. 1999

Protein and fat before sugar

nut bowl + protein dish, 10 minutes before the smoothie

Protein and fat slow stomach emptying and trigger gut hormones that blunt the glucose rise. Eating them first lowered glucose peaks by more than 40% in people with prediabetes.

Source: Shukla et al., Diabetes Care 2015; Diabetes Obes Metab 2019

Protein split into two doses

~26 g in the first meal, ~30 g in the yogurt drink

Muscle protein synthesis peaks at roughly 25–30 g per meal. Spreading protein evenly raised 24-hour muscle protein synthesis by about 25% compared with loading it at dinner.

Source: Paddon-Jones & Rasmussen 2009; Mamerow et al. 2014

Raw crucifers make sulforaphane

raw kaleraw bok choy, blended

Blending releases the enzyme myrosinase, which turns glucosinolates into isothiocyanates (the sulforaphane family). Cooking destroys the enzyme.

Source: Linus Pauling Institute, Isothiocyanates

Yogurt iodine balances raw greens

Greek yogurtegg alongside daily raw crucifers

Large amounts of raw crucifers can slightly interfere with thyroid iodine uptake, mainly when iodine is low. Adequate iodine removes most of the concern. Rock salt is not iodized.

Source: NIH ODS, Iodine fact sheet

Probiotics fed by fiber

yogurt cultures with oat beta-glucanchiaflax

The fiber feeds gut bacteria, and gut bacteria convert flax lignans into their active forms (enterodiol and enterolactone). Keeping the soaking water keeps the soluble beta-glucan.

Source: Linus Pauling Institute, Lignans

Trade-offs that remain:

  • Phytates in nuts, seeds, oats and chia lower zinc absorption, and vitamin C doesn't help with zinc.
  • Egg yolk protein (phosvitin) slightly reduces iron absorption on omelette days. This is minor at 3 eggs a week.

Lunch, tea and dinner

Lemon on every dal

fresh lemon juice on arharrajmachickpeasblack-eyed peas

Legumes are your main iron source. Vitamin C squeezed on just before eating is the best-proven way to raise plant iron absorption, and adding it at the end avoids heat losses.

Source: Hallberg et al. 1989; NIH ODS, Iron fact sheet

Tomatoes cooked in ghee

lots of tomatoes with a little ghee

Cooking tomatoes with fat makes their lycopene much easier to absorb than raw tomato.

Source: Fielding et al. 2005

Raw plate beside the legumes

red bell pepperradishonion with the legume meal

One red bell pepper has well over a day's vitamin C, which adds to the lemon's effect on iron.

Source: USDA FoodData Central; NIH ODS, Iron

Avocado unlocks carotenoids

avocado with red peppercarrot

Adding avocado to a salad raised carotenoid absorption several times over in a human study.

Source: Unlu et al., J Nutr 2005

Tea and chocolate kept apart from iron

tea100% dark chocolate at 4:30 pm

In a human study, black tea cut iron absorption from a meal by 79-94% and cocoa by about 71%. Placing them 4 hours after lunch and 2½ hours before dinner keeps them out of the way.

Source: Hurrell, Reddy & Cook, Br J Nutr 1999

Vegetables before starch

raw plate first, then dal and vegetables, then rice, roti or pasta

The same food-order effect as the nut bowl before the smoothie: fiber and protein first give a gentler glucose rise from the starch.

Source: Shukla et al. 2015, 2019

Cautions

Practical safety notes for this routine.

Fiber

About 26 g in the morning alone and 60–70 g for the whole day, roughly twice the usual target. If that's new for you, build up chia, flax and oats over 1–2 weeks and drink water with them.

Teeth

Lemon water and fruit acids soften enamel. Rinse with plain water afterward and wait about 30 minutes before brushing.

Ginger

Use one ginger route per morning, about 2–3 g fresh. Common guidance is to keep total ginger to about 4 g a day. Check with your doctor if you take blood thinners or have gallstones.

Vitamin K

Kale supplies about 230% of daily needs. If you're ever prescribed warfarin, keep kale intake consistent and tell your doctor.

Chia

Always eat it soaked. Dry chia expands and can stick in the throat.

Calories and glucose

Your nut bowl alone is about 690 calories, and the morning comes to about 1,450–1,500. A default day is roughly 2,300–2,600 calories. If your weight creeps up, the nut bowl (especially the fistful of peanuts) is the easiest place to trim. Check your glucose about 1 hour after the smoothie on a few omelette and non-omelette days.

Dark chocolate

Consumer Reports (2022) found lead and cadmium above California's daily guidance in most of the dark chocolate bars it tested. Keep to about 20 g a day and switch brands now and then.

Tea timing

Keep tea at least 1 hour from lunch, dinner and the smoothie. The planner flags it in red if it's moved closer.

This is general nutrition information, not medical advice. Review it with your doctor or a registered dietitian if you have a medical condition or take regular medication.

Notes & sources

The assumptions behind the numbers, corrections to earlier advice, and the references.

Assumptions

  • Targets are the RDA or AI for men aged 51–70. Calcium and vitamin D targets rise after 70.
  • Greek yogurt is plain and nonfat. Figs are fresh, dates are Medjool, peanuts and pumpkin seeds are unsalted, and oats are rolled.
  • Every menu item shows its portion. Values are calculated from USDA SR28 (kale from SR Legacy 2018), the product labels for Stonyfield yogurt, Lindt 100% and Publix French and sourdough bread, and the USDA-FDA-NIH iodine database.
  • Portions you gave: nut bowl (21 almonds, 7 walnut halves, a fistful of peanuts taken as 28 g, 12 lightly salted cashews, 2 tbsp each pumpkin and sunflower kernels); 2-egg omelette with about 10 shiitake (taken as 100 g) in ½ tsp olive oil; 1½ cups moong sprouts; yogurt drink with 1 cup Stonyfield, 2 heaped tbsp chia (taken as 25 g) and 2 tbsp ground flax; ½ avocado; Publix sourdough slices (56 g); 6 in of Publix French bread (113 g); raw plate with ½ red onion, a 6 in cucumber, a medium red pepper and a 3 in piece of radish (taken as daikon, 100 g); 4 steamed carrots and a slice of beet; Lindt 100%, 2 pieces (taken as 10 g).
  • Assumed until you tell me otherwise: 1 cup of dal is ¾ cup cooked dal plus tomato tadka; 1 cup of rajma, chana or lobia is 0.8 cup cooked beans plus tomato gravy; each cup has about ½ tsp ghee and ¼ tsp iodized salt. Cooked vegetables have 1 tsp ghee and about ⅕ tsp salt per cup. Roti is a 6 in whole-wheat roti (40 g) with no ghee. Rice is white long-grain. Pasta is plain bucatini with jarred marinara, no cheese. Tea is black tea with 2 tbsp 2% milk. Butter is its own menu item.
  • Iodine counts iodized salt, yogurt, eggs and milk only. Some iodine is lost in cooking, so the true figure is probably a bit lower than shown.
  • Nutrient values come from USDA FoodData Central. Kale's calcium value varies between USDA entries, so the calcium figure could be roughly 60 mg lower.

Corrections to earlier advice

  • Besan chilla, tofu scramble and paneer bhurji came out of the weekly plan. They aren't among the foods you cook, so the morning protein is now an omelette or moong sprouts.
  • Apple came out of the smoothie too. The UC Davis study measured apples as high in PPO, like banana.
  • The yogurt drink has about 30 g of protein, not 33 g.
  • Ginger is about 1 tsp grated (2–3 g), not ½ inch, to stay within the 4 g daily guidance.
  • Ayurveda's meal-spacing rule (eat only after the previous meal is digested) was missing from the earlier comparison. It's now listed.

Insights

Ten patterns hidden in this plan, each checked against published research.

49–54%

Your greens beat milk at calcium absorption

About 49% of kale's calcium and 54% of bok choy's is absorbed, compared with about 32% from milk and only about 5% from spinach. Your 120 g of greens likely deliver about as much absorbable calcium as your cup of Greek yogurt, or more.

Heaney & Weaver 1990; Weaver et al. 1999

>40%

Order matters as much as the food

Identical food, eaten in a different order, gave glucose peaks more than 40% lower when protein and vegetables came before carbohydrate. Your nut bowl does this job before the smoothie, at no extra cost.

Shukla et al. 2015, 2019

84%

One banana can undo the berries

A banana smoothie cut flavanol absorption by 84% compared with a berry smoothie. A fruit can be healthy alone and still work against another fruit once they're blended together.

Ottaviani et al. 2023

60 g

Kale alone covers nearly 2 days of vitamin K

Your 60 g of kale supplies about 234 µg of vitamin K, about 195% of the daily need, from a few handfuls of leaves.

USDA FoodData Central

19%

Two spoonfuls, a fifth of your magnesium

Two tablespoons of roasted pumpkin kernels supply about 81 mg of magnesium, roughly 19% of your daily need. Weight for weight, they are among the most mineral-dense foods in your morning, rich in zinc and iron too.

USDA FoodData Central

22 g

Pro-rating changed the verdict

The sugar from figs and dates looked like about 55 g when counted for the whole batch, but your 600 mL serving has about 22 g. Measuring what you actually eat turned a "cut this" into "this is fine."

USDA FoodData Central, calculated

2 traditions

Same advice, different reasons

Ayurveda keeps banana apart from curd as an incompatible food. The PPO research keeps banana apart from berries to protect flavanols. Different mechanisms, but your plan follows both by eating the banana alone.

Viruddha ahara texts; Ottaviani 2023

18%

Where the traditions split: daily yogurt

Ayurveda advises against daily curd. In a Harvard analysis of 3 large cohorts plus a meta-analysis, each daily serving of yogurt was linked to an 18% lower risk of type 2 diabetes. That evidence is observational, so it shows an association, not cause and effect, and it's a genuine point of disagreement.

Chen et al., BMC Medicine 2014

478%

Plenty of omega-3, but not the kind you might think

Chia and flax in the yogurt drink give about 7.7 g of ALA, nearly five times the daily need. But the body converts only a small fraction of ALA into EPA and DHA, the long-chain omega-3s found in fish. Vegetarians who want those use algae oil.

NIH ODS, Omega-3 fact sheet

~0

The Sunshine State gap

Your breakfast has almost no vitamin D. At Boca Raton's latitude, about 26°N, midday sun can make vitamin D in the skin through much of the year, but skin type, age and sunscreen change the amount a lot. Only a blood test tells you your real status.

NIH ODS, Vitamin D fact sheet
